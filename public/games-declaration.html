Games declaration for Google Play

This page is the public declaration for mobile games published by Jupeemoon on the Google Play Store. Use this URL in Play Console where a games / app-content declaration link is required.

Publisher: Jupeemoon Category: Games Last updated: 22 Oct 2026

1. Publisher details

Developer / publisher Jupeemoon
Legal entity Jupeemoon Software Pvt Ltd
Website https://jupeemoon.com
Contact email contact@jupeemoon.com

2. Scope of this declaration

This declaration applies to all Android games published by Jupeemoon on Google Play, including current and future titles released under the Jupeemoon developer account, unless a specific game publishes its own superseding declaration.

  • Casual and mid-core mobile games
  • Kids / educational learning games (where listed as such on Play)
  • Puzzle, skill, and entertainment game apps

3. App category declaration

The apps covered by this page are declared as Games on Google Play. They are designed primarily for interactive entertainment and/or educational play, not as general productivity, news, or social networking applications.

4. Target audience

Target audience is set per game in Google Play Console (content ratings and target age group). Unless a title is enrolled in Designed for Families / specifically marketed to children, our default entertainment games target a general audience as configured in that game’s Play Console listing.

  • General games: age groups selected in Play Console for that title
  • Kids / learning games: age-appropriate design; Families policy applied where enrolled

5. Ads, purchases and monetization

For each game, the following is declared accurately in Google Play Console:

  • Ads: whether the app contains ads (including third-party ad SDKs)
  • In-app purchases: whether the app offers paid digital content or subscriptions through Google Play Billing
  • Paid app vs free: store listing price as shown on Google Play

Where ads are used, they follow Google Play ads and Families policies applicable to that title’s target audience.

6. Data & privacy (summary)

Data collection and sharing for each game is declared in that app’s Data safety form on Google Play. Depending on the title, this may include analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, account data, or purchase history — only as required to operate, improve, or monetize the game and as disclosed in Play Console.

For privacy questions about a specific Jupeemoon game, contact contact@jupeemoon.com with the app name and package name.

7. Content standards

  • No real-money gambling mechanics unless separately declared and licensed
  • No deceptive or impersonation content
  • User-generated content moderated where the game allows it
  • Store listing graphics and descriptions match actual gameplay
  • IARC / content rating questionnaires completed truthfully per title

8. Compliance statement

Jupeemoon affirms that information submitted in Google Play Console for our games — including app category, target audience, ads, in-app purchases, Data safety, and content ratings — is complete and accurate to the best of our knowledge, and that our games are intended to comply with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

9. How to use this URL

Paste this page URL into Google Play Console wherever a public declaration link is requested:

https://jupeemoon.com/games-declaration.html
